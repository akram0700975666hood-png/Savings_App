1. Purpose and Contribution Pacing Rules

The Sunday Savings System is built to coordinate collective member contributions safely and transparently throughout the active annual cycle. Members are expected to contribute what they can afford into the pool tracking register during the scheduled weekly Sunday intervals.

2. Strict Early Withdrawal and Loan Restraints

To safeguard financial blocks and maintain liquidity pool consistency for the community group, no early individual cash outs, partial withdrawals, or provisional personal loans are permitted under any operational circumstances throughout the runtime of this cyclical pool lifecycle layout.

3. Year-End Archive and Payout Schedule

All logged tracking balances will be finalized, compiled into summary datasheets, and officially issued back to individual contributors during the calendar month of December. Following the execution of the global payout rollover sequence by system administrators, active transaction contribution ledger counters will reset back down to zero to initialize the next seasonal lifecycle cycle parameters.
